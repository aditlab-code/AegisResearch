\section*{Abstract}
This document serves as a modular thesis template and practical guide for scientific writing using LaTeX. It covers folder-based chapter organization, figure asset handling within the \texttt{assets/} directory, mathematical typesetting, and automated citation management with BibLaTeX and Biber.

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} LaTeX, Modular Thesis, BibLaTeX, Biber, Scientific Writing.
